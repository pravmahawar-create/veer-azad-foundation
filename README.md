# Veer Azad Charitable Foundation — Enterprise NGO Web Portal & Verification System

Official digital portal, membership management, anti-tamper public certificate issuance, appointment letter (नियुक्ति पत्र) generator, and full content management system (CMS) for **Veer Azad Charitable Foundation**.

---

## 🏛 Statutory Accreditations & Compliance
- **Indian Trusts Act 1882**: Registered Public Charitable Trust (Reg No: `4737/2020`)
- **NITI Aayog NGO Darpan**: Unique ID `UP/2026/03912`

- **Helpline**: `+91 98183 98199`

---

## 🚀 Key Portal Features
1. **Dynamic Top Utility Bar**: Live news marquee ticker, verified 24x7 helpline hotline, live visitor counter (`000109+`), and language switcher.
2. **Active Relief Impact Popup Modal**: Automatic highlight modal with donation progress bar and direct 80G contribution CTA.
3. **Multi-Wing Welfare Structure**: Dedicated divisions for Women Empowerment, Youth Leadership, Education & Healthcare, Social Welfare, and Free Legal Aid.
4. **Public Anti-Tamper Verification**: Instant verification registry via reference number or QR code scan rendering authentic gold-embossed certificates with Chairman Aditya Agrawal's signature and seal.
5. **Appointment Letter Studio (नियुक्ति पत्र)**: Official bilingual appointment letterhead generator with multiple executive themes, official reference tracking, and high-DPI PDF/PNG export.
6. **Statutory Public Resources Archive**: Downloadable verified legal deeds, NITI Aayog certificates, 80G approval orders, bylaws, and printable physical forms.
7. **Full Executive Admin CMS**: Comprehensive Add/Remove management for members, campaigns, resources, team members, news bulletins, and volunteer applications.
